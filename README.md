# Silent Scream Safety Alert App

This is a prototype of my original idea, Silent Scream App, which is a school safety app designed for students. Essentially, the app reports 3 types of alerts: red, orange, and yellow, with red being the utmost urgency to be addressed (fire hazard, bomb, active shooter), orange being intermediate urgency (drug abuse, physical fight), and yellow being not extremely urgent (accident, bullying, or contagion risk). After submission, the alerts can be seen on a "Current Alerts" page, where students can see other reported alerts, their location, time, and description of the alert. 

The red alert contacts the school principal and police, orange contacts the principal, while yellow notifies the counselor. While the red and orange can be submitted within 2 clicks, the yellow requires a 20-word minimum description upon submission. After alert submission, students are given the option to contact 911 or the mental health helpline.
